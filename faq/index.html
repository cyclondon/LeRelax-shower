How do I measure my shower opening?
Measure the width at the top, middle and bottom of the opening (walls are rarely perfectly straight), and the height at both ends. Use the smallest width and the largest height as your working numbers when getting a quote. Our measuring guide walks through this step by step.
What glass thickness should I choose?
This depends on the model and panel size — larger sliding panels typically use 8mm glass for extra rigidity, while smaller or triple-track configurations may use 4-6mm. Each model page lists its standard thickness, and heavier glass options are available where applicable.
What's the difference between tempered and laminated glass?
Tempered (toughened) glass is heat-treated to be several times stronger than ordinary glass, and if it breaks, it shatters into small, relatively blunt pieces rather than sharp shards. It's the standard for most shower screens. Laminated glass adds a plastic interlayer between two glass sheets, so if it breaks, the pieces stay bonded to the interlayer instead of falling apart — offering extra peace of mind, at a higher price. We quote laminated glass separately on request. For more detail, see our full guide to tempered vs laminated glass.

How do I clean and maintain my glass shower screen?
Clean regularly with a soft cloth, water, and a mild detergent, and use a squeegee after each shower to prevent water spots and limescale buildup. Avoid abrasive pads or harsh chemicals, which can damage the glass and hardware over time. For effortless cleaning, ask about our nano-coating treatment — an invisible hydrophobic layer that repels water and soap scum, cutting your regular cleaning time significantly. See our glass surface treatments guide for more on how nano coating works.
