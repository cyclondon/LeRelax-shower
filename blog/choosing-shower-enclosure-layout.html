Sliding, Corner or Quadrant: Choosing the Right Shower Enclosure Layout

This guide is brought to you by 升威 LE-RELAX — Hong Kong shower screen specialists since 1986, and the only company in Hong Kong offering an instant online shower screen configurator and quotation tool. Browse our full range of models or get an instant price any time.

Single-wall sliding screens

If your bathtub or shower area sits flush against a single wall — the most common layout in Hong Kong apartments — a single-wall sliding screen is usually the simplest and most cost-effective option. Panels slide side to side along a track, so no swing space is needed in front of the opening, which matters in smaller bathrooms.

Corner enclosures (square corner)

When your shower area occupies a square or rectangular corner rather than a single wall, a corner enclosure with two glass walls meeting at a right angle is the natural fit. These typically use two sliding doors meeting at the corner, giving a wide, easy-access opening.

Quadrant (rounded corner) enclosures

A quadrant enclosure is a corner design with a curved front edge instead of a sharp corner. The rounded profile softens the look and slightly increases usable floor space just outside the enclosure — a small but genuinely useful benefit in compact Hong Kong bathrooms where every bit of walking space counts.
