Tempered vs Laminated Glass: Which Shower Screen is Right for You?

鋼化玻璃與夾膠玻璃：如何選擇適合的浴屏？

What is tempered glass?

什麼是鋼化玻璃？

Tempered (toughened) glass is regular glass that's been heat-treated to be roughly four to five times stronger than standard glass of the same thickness. It's the standard choice for the vast majority of shower screens — including every model in our range — because it handles daily knocks, water weight, and door-sliding stress well. If tempered glass does break, it shatters into small, relatively blunt granules rather than sharp shards, which is why it's classified as a safety glass.

鋼化玻璃（強化玻璃）是經過熱處理的普通玻璃，強度較同厚度的一般玻璃高出約四至五倍。它是絕大多數浴屏（包括我們所有款式）的標準配置，因為它能承受日常碰撞、水重及趟門移動所帶來的壓力。鋼化玻璃萬一破裂，會碎成細小及較不鋒利的顆粒，而非尖銳碎片，因此被歸類為安全玻璃。

What is laminated glass?

什麼是夾膠玻璃？

Laminated glass is made of two (or more) sheets of glass bonded together with a clear plastic interlayer (usually PVB) in between. If it breaks, the glass pieces stay stuck to the interlayer instead of falling apart — so the pane stays roughly intact even when cracked. This is the same technology used in car windshields.

夾膠玻璃由兩片（或以上）玻璃透過中間的透明塑膠夾層（一般為PVB）黏合而成。萬一破裂，玻璃碎片會黏附於夾層上而不會散落，因此即使出現裂痕，玻璃仍大致保持完整。這與汽車擋風玻璃採用的技術相同。

Which should you choose?

應該如何選擇？

For most Hong Kong households, standard tempered glass is more than sufficient — it's what's fitted as standard across our range, and what the vast majority of customers choose. Laminated glass is worth considering if you have young children, want extra reassurance in a shared or rental property, or simply prefer the added peace of mind. Because laminated glass is heavier and more specialised, we quote it separately on request rather than including it in the standard calculator — just ask your nearest showroom for a laminated glass quote alongside your regular estimate, or give us a call or WhatsApp using the numbers below.

對大部分香港家庭而言，標準鋼化玻璃已經足夠 — 這是我們所有款式的標準配置，亦是絕大多數客戶的選擇。如家中有年幼子女、居於出租或共用物業希望更為安心，或單純想獲得額外保障，則可考慮夾膠玻璃。由於夾膠玻璃較重且屬特殊訂製，我們不會將其納入標準計算工具，而是另行報價 — 歡迎向就近分店查詢夾膠玻璃的報價，以作比較，或致電或 WhatsApp 以下號碼與我們聯絡。
