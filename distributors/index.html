升威 LE-RELAX supplies through a network of over 600 distributors across Hong Kong — just ask for 升威 LE-RELAX! We're a wholesale fabricator: we manufacture and supply, but we don't install directly.

We're wholesale, not an installer
Every shower screen we make is sold through our B2B distributor and trade partner network — the businesses who measure, deliver and install on site for homeowners across Hong Kong. If you're a homeowner looking to have a screen fitted, use the map below to find a distributor near you. If you'd like to become a distributor yourself, get in touch with one of our showrooms below.

How it works
1. We fabricateEvery panel is cut, tempered and finished to order at our own facility — the surface treatments, glass types and hardware you see in our design tool.
2. Distributors sell & installOur trade partners measure your bathroom, place the order with us, and handle delivery and installation on site.
3. You get local supportWarranty, service and installation queries are handled by the distributor you bought from, close to home.
Looking for installation help and can't find a distributor near you on the map? See our FAQ for more on how supply and installation works, or come in and speak to us at one of our own showrooms — see Showrooms for photos, addresses and opening hours.
